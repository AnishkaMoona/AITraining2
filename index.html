01 · Order of the Evening

Agenda & Evening Timeline

Four hours of conversation, seasonal dining, team honors, and friendly competition on the Level 12 Rooftop from 6:00 pm until 10:00 pm.

6:00 pm West Sunset Terrace

Welcome drinks

Ease into the evening with chilled vintage sparkling wine, rosemary-citrus botanical spritzes, craft local lagers, and warm spiced orchard cider served alongside passed artisanal canapés as dusk settles over the skyline.

6:45 pm Glasshouse Dining Pavilion

Dinner

Gather for a seated three-course winter harvest menu featuring pan-roasted sea bass, slow-braised short rib, and wild mushroom truffle risotto, accompanied by shared family-style sides and sommelier-paired wines.

7:45 pm Main Stage & Conservatory

Awards

Raise a glass during our annual Year-End Toast and peer-nominated Golden Compass Awards—celebrating breakout launches, quiet problem-solvers, cross-team mentors, and the most memorable moments of the year.

8:30 pm Sky Lounge & Games Deck

Games

Form your dream squad for fast-paced rooftop trivia, tabletop shuffleboard, and lightning-round music bingo. Extra gold points and curated hampers await the top three teams on the leaderboard.

9:30 pm Gold-Foil Portrait Studio

Photo booth

Step in front of our open-air portrait camera with custom metallic props, instant keepsake prints, and digital gallery downloads while enjoying late-night espresso martinis and warm churros before carriages at 10:00 pm.

02 · Preparation & Style Guide

Dress Code & What to Bring

We want everyone to feel comfortable, festive, and ready for an indoor-outdoor rooftop evening. Keep these three quick tips in mind before heading up to Level 12.

Official Dress Code
Smart casual, with a touch of gold
01. Style Tip

Smart casual, with a touch of gold

Pair relaxed evening tailoring—such as a structured blazer, silk blouse, crisp button-down, or midi dress—with a subtle nod to our theme. No full formalwear required.

Easy ideas: Gold cufflinks, a metallic pocket square, statement earrings, watch, or shimmer scarf.
02. Rooftop Comfort

Pack a warm layer for the terrace

While the Level 12 dining pavilion is glass-enclosed and heated, welcome drinks and sunset views take place on the open sky deck where December evening breezes can be brisk.

Complimentary coat check is staffed right outside the Level 12 elevator lobby all evening.
03. Arrival Essentials

Bring your staff pass & photo ID

Building security switches to after-hours mode at 6:00 pm. Please bring your employee access badge or mobile pass to tap through the ground-floor turnstiles and express rooftop lift.

All food, drinks, game entries, and rideshare return vouchers are fully covered.
03 · Good to Know

Frequently Asked Questions

Click any question below for details on dining accommodations, weather plans, guest policies, and getting home safely.

Our catering kitchen accommodates vegetarian, vegan, gluten-free, dairy-free, halal, and kosher diets, as well as severe nut or shellfish allergies. Simply note your requirements on the RSVP Google Form by Friday 5 December, and a personalized menu card will be waiting at your place setting.

Because seating on the Level 12 Rooftop is tailored to our headcount, the Year-End Team Night is exclusively for team members so everyone can connect across departments. Partners and families are warmly invited to our Annual Mid-Year Family Picnic.

Rain or shine, the celebration goes ahead on Level 12. Two-thirds of the rooftop is covered by a climate-controlled glass conservatory with panoramic city views, and our motorized pergola louvres seal the terrace completely in inclement weather.

Free parking is available in Basement Levels B1 and B2 (vehicles may also be left overnight until Saturday at 2:00 pm if you choose to enjoy a drink). If you are taking a cab or rideshare home, pick up a pre-paid voucher card from the coat-check desk any time after 9:00 pm.
